Step into the ancient world of Nabeul pottery and learn directly from Mohamed Trabelsi, a third-generation master potter whose family has practised this art for over a century. You'll begin by understanding the history of Nabeul's celebrated ceramic tradition before rolling up your sleeves to shape, glaze, and decorate your own piece.

The session takes place in Mohamed's authentic workshop in the medina, surrounded by completed works that showcase the range of what you'll soon be able to create. Every piece you make is yours to take home, wrapped and ready to travel.

Highlights of this experience:

  • Hands-on clay shaping on a traditional wheel
  • Learn traditional Nabeul glazing techniques
  • Decorate your piece with authentic motifs
  • Take your creation home with you
  • Small group — max 8 participants
  • Tea & refreshments included

Included:

  • All materials & tools provided
  • Professional instruction throughout
  • Traditional mint tea & pastries
  • Your finished pottery piece to take home
  • Certificate of participation
  • Workshop apron provided

Not included:

  • Transportation to/from workshop
  • Additional pieces (available to purchase)
